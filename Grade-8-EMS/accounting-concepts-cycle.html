📌 The 5 core building blocks of accounting

ASSET Something the business OWNS
An asset is anything valuable that belongs to the business — something it owns or money owed to it by others.
📌 Examples: Cash, equipment, stock, debtors (people who owe you money), vehicles, buildings
🧠 Trick: "Can I sell it or use it to make money?" → If YES, it's probably an asset.
LIABILITY Something the business OWES
A liability is any debt or obligation — money the business still needs to pay to someone else.
📌 Examples: Bank loan, creditors (businesses you owe), mortgage, overdraft
🧠 Trick: "Does the business owe someone money?" → If YES, it's a liability.
OWNER'S EQUITY What the owner is OWED
Equity is the owner's stake in the business — what's left after all debts are paid. Also called Capital.
📌 Examples: Capital invested, retained profit, drawings (reduces equity)
🧠 Trick: Assets – Liabilities = Equity. It's the owner's "slice" of everything.
INCOME Money the business EARNS
Income (revenue) is money received from selling goods or services. It increases equity.
📌 Examples: Sales, fees received, rent income, interest received
🧠 Trick: "Did the business earn this by doing what it does?" → Income!
EXPENSE Money the business SPENDS
Expenses are the costs of running the business. They reduce profit and therefore reduce equity.
📌 Examples: Rent, salaries, electricity, stationery, advertising, insurance
🧠 Trick: "Did the business spend money to keep running?" → Expense!

⚖️ The golden rule of accounting

The Accounting Equation
Assets
What you OWN
=
Liabilities
What you OWE
+
Equity
Owner's share
⚖️ This equation must ALWAYS balance. Every single transaction keeps it in balance.
🧠 How each element affects the equation
ASSET ↑ Business buys equipment — Assets go UP
LIABILITY ↑ Business takes a loan — Liabilities go UP (and so does Cash asset)
EQUITY ↑ Owner puts in money — Equity (Capital) goes UP
INCOME ↑ Business makes a sale — Income goes UP → Equity goes UP
EXPENSE ↑ Business pays rent — Expense goes UP → Equity goes DOWN

🔄 The accounting cycle — tap each step to expand

The accounting cycle is the step-by-step process every business follows to record, organise and report its financial information. It repeats every accounting period.

🧠 Easy way to remember the order
Identify → Analyse → Record → Post → Balance → Report → Close
